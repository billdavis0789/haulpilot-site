Features

operation needs

AI Phone Intake

Customers call in, AI answers, transcribes, and automatically creates a dispatch job — no human required. Available 24/7.

Drag & Drop Dispatch Board

Visualize your entire day at a glance. Assign drivers and trucks with a drag, see status updates in real time.

How It Works

From phone call to
driver on the road

HaulPilot handles the full dispatch lifecycle — intake, assignment, notification, and tracking.

01

Job Comes In

A customer calls your dispatch number or a dispatcher manually enters a job. AI extracts all the details automatically.

02

Review & Assign

Dispatchers review the job on the live board and drag-and-drop a driver and truck to assign the run.

03

Driver Gets Notified

The driver receives an instant SMS with all job details. They open the mobile portal to confirm and get directions.

04

Job Completed

Status updates flow through the system in real time. Reports are generated automatically at end of day.
